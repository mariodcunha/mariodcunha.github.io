WHAT IT IS
----------
A single self-contained HTML page (index.html: inline CSS + JS, no build step, no
dependencies except the Libre Franklin Google Font). It draws election results as
square cartograms, NYT-style: one square per electoral vote / seat.

  USA   - President 2008-2024 (electoral college; year stepper)
        - House and Senate 2008-2024, every two years. See US HISTORY 2008-2024.
        - Governors 2008-2024 (same years and shared year stepper / chYear as
          House and Senate): the House squares of each state, coloured by the
          governor in office after that November's election (January after;
          odd-year VA/NJ/KY/LA/MS winners count from the next even year).
          GOV_HIST {year:{d, i}}, everyone else R; independents in --ind with
          a named legend entry (Chafee RI 2010-12, Walker AK 2014-16). Totals
          D/R(/I): 08 29/21, 10 20/29/1, 12 19/30/1, 14 18/31/1, 16 16/33/1,
          18 23/27, 20 23/27, 22 24/26, 24 23/27 (typed from memory; totals
          match the published post-election counts);
          square borders take the fill colour so a state reads as one shape.
          Shape size = House seats = population; the bar shows the share of
          House seats in D / R governed states (242 / 193 -> 56% / 44%).
        - ALL FOUR US races share ONE svg (#map-us, drawUS) in one panel
          (#panel-us), so switching tab or year morphs the map: colours change
          and squares grow/shrink in place (scale), all at the same moment,
          .45s, nothing fades (owner, 2026-10-09). President = House squares +
          2 elector squares per state (ESQ, CHAMBER e); Senate circles scale in.
  India - Lok Sabha (543 seats) coloured by alliance, with a year stepper
          (2004, 2009, 2014, 2019, 2024) and Seat share / Vote share (all four
          years). See LOK SABHA 2024 RESULTS and EARLIER ELECTIONS.
        - States: each state's ASSEMBLY seats drawn on its Lok Sabha squares,
          2004-2024. See STATES VIEW.
        - Rajya Sabha: grey Lok Sabha map + one circle per elected RS seat
          (like the Senate view), 2004-2024. See RAJYA SABHA VIEW.

UI: ONE top bar row: race toggle (pill tabs with a sliding thumb), then
#controls = year stepper #year (every race; Governors 2024 only) and
the Seat share / Vote share toggle #ls-share (Lok Sabha only; the owner asked
for no seat/vote share on the USA maps), then the country picker (flag + name
dropdown, USA / India) pushed right. Below: tally numbers + bar with majority
marker, map, legend. Switching country swaps the visible tabs (USA: President,
House, Senate, Governors (this order since 2026-10-09); India: Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha, States) and remembers
the last race per country. URL hash selects the race (#president, #senate,
#house, #governors, #loksabha, #rajyasabha, #states). Always light/white theme.
The row fits at ~730px content width (800px window); narrower, it wraps.


CODE MAP (index.html, top to bottom)
------------------------------------
CSS :root tokens
    --bg #fff, --fg #121212, --muted #6b6b6b, --chip #f2f2f2
    --dem #1f93e6 (same bright blue as --inc), --rep #e5534b (brighter,
    calmer red), --ind #74bcf0 (independents; same as --india). No flip
    hatching anywhere (owner, 2026-10-09: flips not marked on any map).
    --house #e4e4e4 (grey squares behind Senate seats)
    --seat-empty #d3d3d3 (India squares before colouring)
    India groups: --bjp #f26a1b (bold orange), --nda #f7a372 (same orange,
    lighter, solid), --inc #1f93e6 (sky blue), --india #74bcf0 (same blue,
    lighter, solid), --oth #3a9d5d (green). No hatching for India (owner asked
    2026-10-09: allies = solid lighter shade of the party colour).
    (--land / --land-line were deleted.)
Shared map text style:  svg.map text {11px Libre Franklin, weight 500, --muted,
    dominant-baseline central}; text.in = white weight 600 (label inside a block).
#map-india: max-width 940px, centred; its text uses font-size var(--label-size)
    (see "India label size" below).

JS
STATES / DEM / PRES_LABELS - President map: each state as rows
    [row, firstCol, lastCol]; DEM = states won by Harris. ME-2 (r0 c52) and
    NE-2 (r14 c17) split votes are special-cased in drawPresident.
CHAMBER - House + Senate, one object per state:
    s state, c House squares [x,y,isDem], o two Senate seat squares,
    p Senate seat parties (d/r/i), h label [text, x, y] (centred).
US names (both maps, owner's rule 2026-10-09): ALL outside the blocks.
    Multi-word -> capitals, no dots: NH NJ NY NM RI DC. Directions -> N-Dako.
    S-Dako. W-Virg. N-Caro. S-Caro. 4-6 letters as is (Texas, Maine, Ohio,
    Kansas, Oregon, Nevada, Alaska, Hawaii, Idaho, Iowa, Utah). Longer ->
    first 4 letters + "." (Penn. Mass. Mont. Verm. Conn. Loui. Cali. ...),
    except Missouri "Misso." / Mississippi "Missi." (both would be "Miss.").
US layout spread 15% (2026-10-09): each state block shifted by
    round(centre * 0.15) squares (shapes unchanged) to make room for the
    names; names placed by a greedy search (above, below, beside the block;
    0.08 clear of own squares, 0.3 of other states' squares and Senate
    circles, 0.2 between names), checked in the browser: no overlaps.
fitBox(b, labels) sizes both US viewBoxes from squares + names.
S=20 (SVG units per square), el(), square(),
    label(parent, [text, x, y, insideOrAnchor, anchor]) - shared helpers.
    Labels: a===1 means inside (white, centred); a string is the text-anchor.
drawUS (builds HSQ House squares incl. older-census extras, ESQ elector
    squares, SEAT Senate circles; HOUSE_COL colours per year) / countTo /
    setUS(race, animate, year) paints the shared map for any US race.
    (Old drawPresident / drawChamber / drawGovernors are gone.)
INDIA + drawIndia - Lok Sabha map, see next section.
Race toggle (show, moveThumb, panelOf, countryOf, lastRace) and Country picker
    (setCountryUI, openMenu, choose; keyboard accessible listbox).

Figma frame exactly. Imported three times so far (latest 2026-10-09, frame now

Data: const INDIA = [[code, name, cells, [labelX, labelY, anchor]], ...]
    code   two-letter state/UT code (UP, MH, WB, ... DD = DNH & Daman-Diu)
    name   label text
    cells  [[x, y], ...] top-left corner of each seat square, in square units
           (multiply by S=20 for SVG units). Values are fractional: every state
           block keeps its exact Figma position, and blocks are not on one shared
           grid (each has its own sub-square offset). Inside a state the squares
           are on a whole-unit grid.
    label  [x, y, anchor]; anchor "middle" | "start" | "end".
Seat counts (total 543): UP 80, MH 48, WB 42, BR 40, TN 39, MP 29, KA 28, GJ 26,
    RJ 25, AP 25, OD 21, KL 20, TS 17, AS 14, JH 14, PB 13, CG 11, HR 10, DL 7,
    JK 5, UK 5, HP 4, GA 2, AR 2, MN 2, ML 2, TR 2, DD 2, and 1 each for LA, CH,
    SK, NL, MZ, PY, AN, LD.
Seat counts are verified: squares per state == the counts above (36 states/UTs,
    543 total, no duplicate squares). History by election: see LOK SABHA SEATS
    BY ELECTION below.
Label names: full names for most; shortened: J.&K., Himachal, W. Bengal,
    Andhra, Arunachal, A&N Islands, DNH-DD (Dadra & Nagar
    Haveli and Daman & Diu; owner asked for "DNH-DD").
Labels: svg.india text = 14 SVG units (11 -> 12.5 -> 14 on 2026-10-09; the
    US maps stay 11). "Chhattisgarh" spelled out (was "C.G."); Himachal's name
    moved beside its block ([14.5, 3.8, "start"]) to clear J&K. Checked in the
    browser: no name touches a square or another name. The owner said small is fine as long as names never
    cross each other or a square. Placement (done offline, in square units):
    try centred above the block's top row (0.42 / 0.7 / 1.0 up), then below the
    bottom row, shifting sideways by up to 2 squares, then beside the block
    (start/end anchored); first spot whose text box (measured width at 11px,
    0.59 tall) keeps 0.08 clear of every square and 0.2 clear of every placed
    label wins. Biggest states are placed first. Verified in the browser: no
    overlaps, nothing clipped.
No hover effect: the owner tried one (name darker/bigger on hover) and asked
    for it to be removed - names just stay small and static.
Drawing: squares are <rect class="seat-sq"> (fill --seat-empty #d3d3d3, 1px
    white stroke); no outlines. viewBox fitted to squares plus approximate
    label extents (name.length * 0.32 units wide).
The earlier "same on-screen size as the House labels" sizing (--label-size +
    ResizeObserver) was removed with this change.

US HISTORY 2008-2024 (added 2026-10-09)
---------------------------------------
Years: President 2008/2012/2016/2020/2024 (presYear, PRES_YEARS);
  House + Senate every two years 2008-2024 (chYear, CH_YEARS, shared by both
  tabs so House <-> Senate keeps the year).
Seat counts per census: House seats (and electoral votes = House + 2) per
  state change with the census. CENSUS_DIFF = changes from today's (2020
  census) counts: 2000 census for 2008/2010, 2010 census for 2012-2020.
  Squares per state are kept in a fill order: today's squares row by row
  (serp snake), then EXTRA squares (PRES_EXTRA / CHAMBER[].x) for states that
  had more seats before (NY +3, OH +3, IL/MI/PA +2, CA IA LA MA MO NJ WV +1).
  A year shows the first n squares and fades the rest out (class "gone"), so
  states that had fewer seats (TX, FL, AZ...) lose squares from the bottom
  row. Extras were placed next to each block, >= 1 empty square from other
  states; names re-placed over all squares so none collide in any year.
President: PRES {year: {d, r (names), dem (states won by the Democrat),
  me2, ne2 (Maine / Nebraska 2nd districts)}}. Totals (by state won):
  2008 Obama 365 / McCain 173; 2012 Obama 332 / Romney 206; 2016 Clinton 232
  / Trump 306 (legend notes the faithless electors: official 227 / 304);
  2020 Biden 306 / Trump 232; 2024 Harris 226 / Trump 312. Checked: 538 each.
House: HOUSE_HIST {year: {state: [D, R]}} 2008-2022 (2024 = CHAMBER c).
  Colours chain back from 2024: each earlier year starts from the next
  year's colours and changes as few squares as possible (HOUSE_COL), so the
  squares are per state, not per district. Vacant = --house grey (only NC-9
  2018, legend shows "Vacant").
  Totals D/R: 2008 257/178, 2010 193/242, 2012 201/234, 2014 188/247,
  2016 194/241, 2018 235/199 (+1 vacant), 2020 222/213, 2022 213/222,
  2024 215/220 - all match the official results.
Senate: SEN_HIST {year: {state: "dr"}}, one letter per seat in class order
  (circle 0 = lower class), d / r / i. Snapshot = 20 days after the new
  Congress began (so 2020 includes the Georgia runoff winners; 2008 adds
  Franken (MN) and Gillibrand (NY), seated later). Totals D+I / R: 2008 59/41,
  2010 53/47, 2012 55/45, 2014 46/54, 2016 48/52, 2018 47/53, 2020 50/50
  (note: VP tie-break, check on Democrats), 2022 51/49 (Sinema = i),
  2024 47/53.
Net gains (CH_GAIN, Wikipedia infobox "seat change"): House D+21, R+63, D+8,
  R+13, D+6, D+41, R+13, R+9, D+2; Senate D+8, R+6, D+2, R+9, D+2, R+2, D+3,
  D+1, R+4 (2008 -> 2024). CONTROL = party with the check mark.
Source: github.com/unitedstates/congress-legislators (legislators-current
  + legislators-historical JSON): every House term starting within days of
  each Congress's first day, every Senate term covering the snapshot date.
  Seats vacant on day one were credited to the November winner: IL-5 2008
  (Emanuel), IL-2 + SC-1 2012, NY-11 2014, LA-5 + NY-22 2020, VA-4 2022,
  FL-1 2024. Script: build.py in the session scratchpad (stdlib only).
  President winners typed from the official results (sums checked).


LOK SABHA 2024 RESULTS (seat share / vote share)
-----------------------------------------------
Owner's spec: only five groups -
    BJP                      bold orange, solid            (--bjp)
    BJP-aligned (NDA allies) lighter orange, solid         (--nda)
    Congress (INC)           sky blue, solid               (--inc)
    Congress-aligned (INDIA) lighter blue, solid           (--india)
    Not aligned / others     green                         (--oth)
Group order everywhere (bar, data arrays, square filling) = LS_GROUPS =
    [bjp, nda, oth, india, inc]  -> NDA on the left, others in the middle,
    INDIA on the right (like Democrats left / Republicans right in the US).

UI (panel-loksabha):
  Tally: left NDA total (orange, check mark = won majority, hidden in vote
    view), centre Others (green, smaller), right INDIA total (blue). Under
    each: "BJP n · allies n" / "Congress n · allies n" (two lines under 520px).
  Bar: five solid segments in group order. Marker + "272 FOR MAJORITY" shown in seat view,
    faded out in vote view.
  Controls live in the top bar (#controls), not in the panel:
    .stepper#year = year stepper, same pill look as the race toggle:
      [<] [ 2024 ] [>]. Left arrow = earlier election, right = later; an
      arrow is disabled at the end of the list (at 2024 the right arrow).
      The year sits in a white window on a 3D drum (10-sided, 36 deg per
      step, CSS rotateX + translateZ, top/bottom masked) that rolls like a
      meter: going back, the earlier year drops in from above. Code:
      LS_YEARS, US_YEARS, setStepper(years, year, animate, onPick).
      (Replaced the short-lived drag lever on the owner's request.)
    .subtoggle = "Seat share" / "Vote share" pill, same look and height as the
      race toggle (aria-pressed).
  Map: each state's squares ordered row by row from the top, snaking (left to
    right, then right to left; serp()), then filled in group order - owner
    asked for horizontal filling with each colour kept in one patch (was
    column by column). Same order on the States map and for the Rajya Sabha
    circles (which sit on the top rows of each state). Filled with counts from LS[code].s (seat view) or
    LS[code].q (vote view, 2024; LS_VOTE_HIST for older years). Fill =
    var(--bjp) / var(--nda) / var(--oth) / var(--india) / var(--inc).
    Fill transitions .45s with a small delay by row (a top-to-bottom wipe).
  Legend: 5 swatches + a note that changes per view.
  Code: LS, LS_GROUPS, LS_HIST, LS_VOTE_HIST, LS_VOTE_NAT, LS_META, LS_RECTS,
    drawIndia, lsSeats(year), lsRegionRects(code, regions),
    setLokSabha(mode, animate, year), LS_YEARS + setStepper (year stepper).
    countTo / countToPct cancel a running count (node._raf) before starting.

Numbers (2024):

Vote-share squares (owner's rule): for each state, the five group shares
  (NOTA removed, re-normalised to 100) become squares by the largest-remainder
  method so the counts add up to the state's seats. States with 1 or 2 seats:
  all squares go to the group with the highest vote share (e.g. Goa 2 x BJP,
  Meghalaya 2 x others, Sikkim 1 x others because SKM's 43% is below
  CAP-S + SDF + independents at 51%).
  "Others" in a state = 100 - NOTA - (BJP + NDA allies + INC + INDIA allies),
  i.e. every party not in NDA/INDIA plus independents.

Data sources and how it was built (repeatable):
  1. Seats per party per state: the coloured summary bar in each state section
     of en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Results_of_the_2024_Indian_general_election
     (wikitext bold "PARTY (n)" cells). Bihar's CPI(ML) 2 seats had to be
     added by hand (brackets broke the regex). Check: each state sums to its
     seat count.
  2. Vote share per party per state: "2024 Indian general election in <State>"
     pages, rendered HTML via the MediaWiki parse API, read with
     pandas.read_html; pick the table whose "%"/"share" column has party
     abbreviations incl. BJP/INC and sums closest to 100. J&K's page picked a
     constituency table, so its values came from its alliance table:
     JKNC 22.30, BJP 24.36, INC 19.38, JKPDP 8.48, NOTA 0.67.
  3. National totals + alliance lists: "Results by party and alliances" table
     on the results page.
  Wikipedia rate-limits (HTTP 429): fetch with ~3s gaps and retry/backoff.
  The ECI results site (results.eci.gov.in/PcResultGenJune2024) is offline.
  Caveat: Wikipedia, not the ECI, is the source; spot-check before quoting.
Older elections: see LOK SABHA SEATS BY ELECTION for the state merges needed.


EARLIER ELECTIONS: 2019, 2014, 2009 (seat share and vote share)
---------------------------------------------------------------
UI: the year stepper in the top bar (see above). Both views work for every year
    (vote share for 2009-2019 added 2026-10-09; 2004 seats + vote share added
    later the same day at the owner's request - 2004 is now the earliest).
Owner's rule: keep the 2024 map and recolour it for older years:
    - J&K + Ladakh = ONE 6-seat region in 2009-2019 (fills the 5 J&K squares
      and the Ladakh square together).
    - Andhra Pradesh + Telangana = ONE 42-seat region in 2009 and 2014
      (Telangana was created 2 June 2014, after the 2014 count); separate
      in 2019 and 2024.
    - DNH-DD: always two separate seats. WESTERN square = Daman & Diu (DM),
      EASTERN square = Dadra & Nagar Haveli (DN). 2024 is split the same way:
      DM = independent (Umeshbhai Patel), DN = BJP (Kalaben Delkar).
    Labels stay as on the 2024 map. Legend note says "drawn on today's state
    map" for older years.
Names per year: the Congress-led bloc is INDIA in 2024 and UPA in 2009-2019
    (tally label and legend change). Check mark = alliance that formed the
    government: NDA in 2024/2019/2014, UPA in 2009 (UPA 262 < 272, governed
    with outside support).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Live briefly on 2026-10-09 (commit 6941717), then replaced: the owner prefers
the hand-made Figma layout. Method, in case real-geography packing is wanted:
Inputs: block shapes (Appendix A) + real state centroids (lat, lon):
    JK 33.6,74.9  LA 34.4,77.6  HP 31.9,77.2  PB 30.9,75.4  CH 30.73,76.78
    HR 29.1,76.1  DL 28.65,77.2 UK 30.1,79.2  UP 26.9,80.9  RJ 26.6,73.8
    GJ 22.7,71.6  MP 23.5,78.3  CG 21.3,82.0  BR 25.9,85.6  JH 23.6,85.3
    WB 23.8,87.9  OD 20.5,84.4  MH 19.4,76.1  TS 17.9,79.0  AP 15.9,79.7
    KA 14.7,76.2  GA 15.4,74.0  KL 10.4,76.4  TN 11.0,78.4  PY 11.9,79.8
    SK 27.5,88.5  AS 26.2,92.8  AR 28.0,94.5  NL 26.1,94.4  MN 24.7,93.8
    MZ 23.3,92.8  TR 23.7,91.7  ML 25.5,91.3  DD 20.6,72.9  LD 10.6,72.6
    AN 11.7,90.0 (pulled west from 92.7 so it is not miles offshore)
Target: t = K * (lon * 0.927, -lat), K = 2.0 squares per degree.
Constraints (hard): no two states' squares within one square of each other
    (Chebyshev distance >= 2); label box (measured text width at 15.7 SVG units,
    0.85 tall, centred one row above the top row) may not touch another state's
    squares, and labels keep 0.15 apart.
Cost (minimised):
    sum over state pairs within 10 degrees of
        w_ab * | (centre_a - centre_b) - (t_a - t_b) |^2
        w_ab = 1/(dist_deg + 1) * (n_a * n_b)^0.25      (keeps relative layout)
  + 3 * sum over real land neighbours of max(0, gap - 1)^2   (uniform 1 gap)
    Neighbour list: JK-LA JK-HP JK-PB LA-HP HP-PB HP-CH HP-HR HP-UK PB-CH PB-HR
    PB-RJ CH-HR HR-DL HR-UP HR-RJ DL-UP UK-UP UP-RJ UP-MP UP-CG UP-JH UP-BR
    RJ-GJ RJ-MP GJ-MP GJ-MH GJ-DD DD-MH MP-MH MP-CG CG-MH CG-TS CG-AP CG-OD
    CG-JH BR-JH BR-WB JH-WB JH-OD OD-WB OD-AP MH-TS MH-KA MH-GA GA-KA KA-TS
    KA-AP KA-KL KA-TN TS-AP AP-TN TN-KL TN-PY WB-SK WB-AS AS-AR AS-NL AS-ML
    AS-MN AS-MZ AS-TR AR-NL NL-MN MN-MZ MZ-TR
Search: start from the Figma arrangement spread out (x1.64) until legal, then
    simulated annealing (12,000 steps, T 20 -> 0): pick a block, shift it one
    square in one of 8 directions, pushing any blocks it collides with along
    (up to 8 blocks); accept by Metropolis rule. Ran several random seeds, kept
    the best (seed 5). Starting from a greedy placement instead gave wrong
    topology (Bihar above UP, Chhattisgarh below Odisha), so start from Figma.
Hand polish after the search (to close central voids): MP 2 left, CG 3 left
    and 2 up, JH 1 left, OD 2 left. Then labels checked in the browser.
A gravity term (pull to the centre) was tried and rejected: it moved Delhi above
    Haryana and dragged the Andamans inland.


APPENDIX A: HOW THE FIGMA BLOCK SHAPES WERE IMPORTED (repeatable)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Use this to re-import after the owner edits the Figma frame, or to build a tile
map for another country the same way.
1. Figma frame: one child frame per state named "<CODE>-<slug> 1"
   (e.g. "UP-uttar-pradesh 1"); squares are ~78.7px with a ~82.87px pitch.
   get_metadata gives each state frame's bbox, but some state frames are
   flattened (no per-square children), so do not count squares from metadata.
2. get_screenshot of the frame at 1:1 (maxDimension = frame height, ~5329).
   Detect squares by colour (grey ~rgb(165,163,153) on a ~#e5e5e5 background)
   with connected-component labelling (scipy.ndimage.label); keep components
   taller than 20px. Expect exactly 543.
3. Assign each square to the state frame whose bbox contains its centre.
   Better: group squares into connected blocks (edge or diagonal neighbours,
   pitch < 1.2) and give each block the majority state of its clean (single-
   frame) squares. Blocks with no clean squares were named by position.
   Second import: J&K = 5 at the top, Uttarakhand = 5 squares east of Delhi
   (frame unnamed), Manipur = 2 inside the Assam frame, Goa = 2 left of
   Karnataka, Puducherry = 1 inside the Andhra frame, Andaman = lone square
   east of Tamil Nadu, Lakshadweep = lone square far left ("Frame 1660").
   Third import (same rules plus): Telangana's frame came through unnamed, so a
   block is only given its majority state when that state covers at least
   half the block; otherwise it is named by size/position (17 squares = TS,
   5 squares not at the top = UK). Never let 2 stray MH-frame squares claim TS.
   First import: J&K = the 5 at the very top, Goa = 2 squares
   left of Karnataka, Lakshadweep = lone square far left, Manipur = 2 squares
   overlapping the Assam frame, Meghalaya = 2 squares overlapping the WB frame,
   Bihar/W.Bengal squares in each other's frames go to their own block.
4. Convert px to square units (divide by 82.8681, subtract 0.5 for the corner).
   Per state, take the circular mean of the fractional x and y offsets, snap the
   squares to whole units around that offset, round to 0.01, then shift
   everything so the minimum x/y is 0.
   (This is exactly how the CURRENT India data was produced.)
5. Check: per-state counts (list above), total 543, then in the browser compare
   every <text> getBoundingClientRect against every rect and every other text
   (no overlaps), and that no label pokes outside the SVG.
Python used: a scratch venv with pillow, numpy and scipy.
