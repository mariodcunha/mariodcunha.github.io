Sunday · June 28

SF Pride

The main event — parade day. Catch a couple hours, leave when you've had your fill.

8:24 AM

Park at Mountain View, ride Caltrain in

Leave the car at Mountain View Caltrain station and take the train straight to SF (4th & King) — no driving into the closures. Catch the 8:24 AM (arrives ~9:25) or the 8:54 AM (arrives ~9:55); they run about every 30 min. Have your breakfast & coffee on board — the ride's about an hour.

Train notes

  • From 4th & King, the Muni T-line (Central Subway) or a quick rideshare gets you to Union Square / Market St for the parade.
  • Mountain View station parking is generally free on weekends — but double-check the signage.
  • June 2026 has World Cup matches in the Bay Area; confirm the exact Sunday times in the Caltrain app a day or two before.
10:30 AM

Pride Parade — Market Street → Civic Center

Steps off at 10:30 and rolls up Market, ending at the Civic Center Plaza celebration. Catch a couple of hours, then peel off when you've had your fill — you'll be ready for a proper sit-down by lunch.

Pack light — there are entry checkpoints

  • ID, phone, sunscreen, an empty reusable water bottle
  • Layers / a jacket — SF mornings run cold (55–60°F) even with sun later
  • No bag, or a small clear / clutch-sized one only — speeds up entry

Civic Center light beams + home

If the Pride light beams are lit over Civic Center Plaza, loop back for a few photos before heading home.

Getting home — read this before the evening

  • The last southbound Caltrain leaves SF around 9:45–10:00 PM (reaching Mountain View ~10:50). The full cocktails → dinner → piano bar → light-beams night runs well past that.
  • So plan to rideshare back to the car / Mountain View (~$90–120, ~50 min) for the late night — or, if you'd rather the train, do an early dinner, skip the late Martuni's, and catch that ~9:45 train.
